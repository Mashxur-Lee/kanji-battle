    How to play

    1. Create a room, pick a mode, and send the 4-letter code to a friend. They tap Join. On a phone you can switch apps to send the code — your seat is kept for 5 minutes.
    2. Pick your levels. かな is hiragana (answer in romaji); N5 is easiest, N1 hardest. Harder words hit harder, so your opponent gets more HP — matches stay fair across levels.
    3. Study. You get 10 words and 60 seconds. Press I'm ready to start early.
    4. Battle. The readings vanish. Each word becomes a spell:
      • Reading — type the reading with a Japanese keyboard (かな) or in romaji, then Enter.
      • Writing — the kanji flashes for half a second, then only the meaning stays. Draw each character on the pad and press Next. Neat-ish is enough.
      • Boss — you and a friend (or solo) fight the Black Dragon. Correct answers hit it. A mistake gets you clawed, and every 30 s it breathes fire on everyone.
    5. Fast + correct = more damage. Answers in a row build a combo (up to ×1.5). A miss resets it.
    6. Don't know it? Press Skip (or Esc): it counts as a miss and shows you the answer, and that word comes back soon.
    7. After the battle you'll see what you struggled with — that's your study list.
