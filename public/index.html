    How to play

    1. Create a room, pick a mode, and send the 4-letter code to a friend. On a phone you can switch apps to send it — your seat is kept for 5 minutes.
    2. Pick your levels. かな is hiragana (romaji answers); N5 is easiest, N1 hardest. Your character follows your hardest level: Goblin (かな), Apprentice (N5), Adventurer (N4), Knight (N3), Wizard (N2/N1). Harder words hit harder, so your opponent gets more HP.
    3. Modes
      • Reading — study 10 words, then type each reading (かな or romaji).
      • Rapid — no study; both get the same kanji, first correct reading hits.
      • Writing — the kanji flashes, then write it on the pad or type the kanji with a Japanese keyboard. Messy is fine.
      • Boss — 1 to 4 players team up against the Black Dragon (it gets tougher with every player). Mistakes get you clawed; fire breath every 30 s.
      • Deck Duel — pick a hero, draft cards by colour, then cast them by writing their kanji. Light blue 100 dmg (10 mana), blue 120 (25), yellow +60 mana, green heal 100 (40), red 250 (70). Both players press Ready to start. 15 s to choose a card, then 20 s to write it. You get +10 mana each turn. Out of cards → a new draft round (HP and mana stay). Cards left but no mana to play any → you lose. When time runs out: overtime — cards are shown one by one, first to write it uses it.
    4. Fast + correct = more damage. Combos build up to ×1.5 (5 in a row sets you on fire 🔥). Skip (Esc) shows the answer.
    5. Study spells — Anki-style flashcards. Missed words go to Struggling spells. Crit: 1% + 1% per spell learned today (max 50%, resets daily; a crit hits ×1.5).
    6. XP — win: 300 + accuracy%; loss: 50 + accuracy%; Deck Duel: 4000 / 1500. Forfeited matches give no XP. Each level needs 1000 XP more than the last (1000, then 2000, 3000 …) and unlocks backgrounds in Customize.
