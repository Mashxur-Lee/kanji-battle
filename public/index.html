Get the app

The app opens straight into the game and works offline too: Study spells, the JLPT quiz, reading passages, the daily challenge, Customize, Progress and match history. Online queue and private games need internet. Your account and progress are the same everywhere.

Phone or PC — from this browser

No store needed: it installs like an app, with its own icon.

On iPhone / iPad (Safari): tap Share ⎙ → Add to Home Screen.

In Chrome or Edge: open the browser menu ⋮ → Install app (or Add to Home screen).

Windows

Download and run the installer. If Windows says “protected your PC”, choose More info → Run anyway (the app isn't signed yet).

Privacy

We store your player name, a scrambled (hashed) password, your progress, match history, friends and — if you upload one — your profile picture. Bug reports and suggestions you send are kept with your player name; errors include your browser type. Nothing is sold or shared. Ask the admin to delete your account and all of it is removed.

      Reading passage

      A short story with your words left out. Tap a blank, then the word that fits — the story around it is the clue. Tap any word for its meaning, and a kanji in it for its parts.

      How reading passages work

      • An AI writes one short story with 8 of your words in it — each word appears where it makes sense, not in a sentence of its own.
      • The words are blanked out. Tap a blank, then the word that fits from the context. Check shows what you got right.
      • Writing a story takes a few seconds — the bar shows how far along it is.
      • You can have up to 10 new AI stories a day — the counter next to New passage shows how many are left. After that (or offline), the passage uses your words’ example sentences instead.

          How to play

          1. Create a room, pick a mode, and send the 4-letter code to a friend. The battle starts when every player presses Ready. On a phone you can switch apps to send it — your seat is kept for 5 minutes.
          2. Pick your levels. ひらがな (hiragana) and カタカナ (katakana) are the kana letters (romaji answers); N5 is easiest, N1 hardest. Your character follows your hardest level: Goblin (ひらがな / カタカナ), Apprentice (N5), Adventurer (N4), Knight (N3), Witch (N2), Wizard (N1). Everyone holds the magic staff they equipped in Customize. Harder words hit harder, so your opponent gets more HP.
          3. Modes
            • Reading — study 10 of your words, then type each reading (かな or romaji).
            • Rapid — no study; both get the same kanji, first correct reading hits. The first 10 are 5 words from each player’s All spells — the round shows whose word it is.
            • Writing — look at the kanji, press CAST! (up to 3.5 s), then write it from memory on the pad or type it with a Japanese keyboard. Messy is fine.
            • Boss — 1 to 4 players team up against the Black Dragon (it gets tougher with every player). Mistakes get you clawed; fire breath every 30 s.
            • Deck Duel — pick a hero, draft cards by colour, then cast them by writing their kanji. Bolt 90 dmg (10 mana), Frost 150 (20), Mana +60, Heal 140 (30), Inferno 340 (45). 15 s to choose a card, then 1 minute to read its meaning, look at the kanji and write it. +10 mana each turn. Out of cards → a new draft round (HP and mana stay). No mana for any of your cards → your turn is skipped (−100 HP). When time runs out: overtime, a Rapid duel. Every kanji of the duel joins your All spells.
          4. Your own words — every battle plays the words of your levels from your All spells (marked ★ on the study cards); random words fill in when you have too few.
          5. Fast + correct = more damage. Combos build up to ×1.5 (5 in a row sets you on fire 🔥). Skip (Esc) shows the answer.
          6. Study spells — Anki-style flashcards in four decks: All spells, Struggling spells (the words you miss go there), Custom and Onomatopoeia. In All spells, set how many new words of each level you get a day (− / +, then Apply); Onomatopoeia has its own “New a day” number (0 until you set it). Every card you answer gives +5 XP (up to 500 a day); ↶ Undo takes the last answer back.
          7. Practice — the JLPT quiz, the Onomatopoeia quiz and the Reading passage: an AI writes one short story with 8 of your words, and you fill in the blanks from the context (up to 10 new stories a day — the counter shows how many are left; ★ starred words go in first, and the words you miss go to Struggling spells). Tap any kanji for its parts and a memory story.
          8. Dictionary (辞書, main menu, works offline) — type a word in kanji, kana, romaji, English or Russian: every meaning, its JLPT level, verb forms, an example, each kanji with its memory picture, other words with that kanji — and “+ Add to…” puts it in any deck.
          9. Combo pose — in Customize, choose what your character does while on fire (5 in a row, or Omnipotence in Deck Duel): Channel, Duelist or Levitation — they unlock at 100, 200 and 300 spells learned. Everyone in the battle sees it.
          10. Kana — hiragana and katakana have their own section, Kana (main menu): a chart that fills in as you master each one, and practice by rows — new kana are shown first, then asked with look-alike answers until each has its stars. Beginners battle with ひらがな until they tick “I’ve mastered hiragana” in Settings (mastering the 46 basic hiragana ticks it for you).
          11. XP — win: 300 + accuracy%; loss: 50 + accuracy%; Deck Duel: 4000 / 1500. Forfeited matches give no XP. Each level needs 1000 XP more than the last (1000, then 2000, 3000 …) and unlocks backgrounds and flame colours in Customize.
          12. Daily streak — a day counts when you do all three daily tasks: study 10 spells, a JLPT quiz (7/10) or a reading passage (4/8), and the daily challenge. Your best streak unlocks magic staffs in Customize: Ember at 3 days, Tide at 5, Storm at 8, Void at 10.
